Standardized Physical Inspection Sequence

Execute steps in strict chronological order to isolate mechanical vs electronic valve stall.

EST. DURATION: 45 MIN
01
Pre-Inspection Safe State & Isolation Lockout MANDATORY GATE

Remove the Manual Service Disconnect (MSD) orange plug located beneath the rear seat luggage bulkhead. Apply safety padlock tag-out. Allow exactly 300 seconds (5 min) for the intermediate circuit DC bus capacitors to discharge through the internal bleeding resistor bank.

verified Threshold Gate: Verify < 5.0 V DC across auxiliary inverter terminals using CAT IV 1000V probe before proceeding.
STATUS: UNVERIFIED
02
Visual & Acoustic Leak / Rotor Stall Check CHILLER HOUSING

Access front wheel well liner (LH side, forward of splash shield). Inspect proportional valve casing VAG 9J1.965.561.A for white-pink silicate crystallization (G40 additive leakage). Power low-voltage 12V terminal and cycle ignition; place mechanic's stethoscope on valve dome to detect stepper motor calibration sweep.

hearing Acoustic Sign: Three repetitive rapid clicks indicates internal gearbox tooth stripping or rotor mechanical binding.
03
Resistance & PWM Pinout Verification HARNESS X832

Disconnect 4-pin harness plug X832. Using calibrated digital ohmmeter at ambient 20°C, probe between Pin 1 (+Stepper Coil A) and Pin 2 (-Stepper Coil B). Verify nominal resistance. Connect oscilloscope channel to Pin 3 (Duty cycle command from Thermal Master ECU) to read square-wave PWM.

Nominal Coil Resistance
18.5Ω – 22.0Ω (@ 20°C)
PWM Control Frequency
100 Hz ± 5 Hz (12V Peak-to-Peak)
04
Hydraulic Loop Evacuation & PIWIS Bleed Routine VACUUM BLEED

Following any plumbing intervention or mechanical valve reset, connect shop coolant evacuation rig. Pull vacuum down to -0.85 bar for minimum 8 minutes to verify zero micro-fissure leakage. Refill with 50/50 G40 demineralized coolant. Trigger PIWIS III thermal bleeding script (Routine 19-B-04).

QUEUED
05
Post-Repair Telemetry Re-Validation FINAL PASS GATE

Re-engage MSD safety disconnect lock. Boot vehicle into Diagnostics Service Mode. Command 100% chiller loop duty cycle for 120 seconds. Monitor live live sensor streams in Auto Sage telemetry canvas: Inflow Temp vs Return Delta must exceed 6.5°C with pump flow confirmed at > 12.2 L/min.

PENDING STEPS 1-4

Critical Technician Rule: Do Not Condemn Valve Prematurely

If physical inspection confirms nominal coil resistance (18.5Ω–22.0Ω) and free rotor articulation using Special Tool T40321, do not replace the valve body. Ground stud G104 (front body harness near LH suspension turret) is prone to oxidation, creating intermittent ground float that mimics an actuator jam during heavy chiller demand.

build Scope 1: Harness / Ground Rework

Clean and re-torque ground point G104 to 9.0 Nm. Inspect pin crimps on Connector X832 for pin retraction or fretting corrosion. Apply Krytox lubricant to seal.

APPROX. TIME: 0.5 HR • NO FLUID LOSS
swap_horiz Scope 2: Valve Actuator Replacement

Requires full vacuum drain of Chiller Circuit B. Remove bracket bolts, decouple quick-release coolant lines with hose clamps, install replacement 9J1.965.561.A.

APPROX. TIME: 1.8 HR • PIWIS BLEED MANDATORY

Dealership & Certified Facility Escalation Criteria

Defines hard stop boundaries where independent workshops must escalate to Porsche Certified High-Voltage Centers.

electric_meter
1. Voltage Isolation Failure Post-Lockout

Residual voltage measured across the auxiliary contactors exceeds 5.0V DC after 5 minutes of MSD pull. Indicates internal inverter capacitor discharge resistor failure or contactor weld. Do not touch any wiring.

SEVERITY: CRITICAL HAZARD • ESCALATE IMMEDIATELY
link_off
2. HVIL Open-Circuit Loop Fault

High-Voltage Interlock Loop (HVIL) fault codes (e.g., P0C73) persist, indicating physical rupture or high resistance along the perimeter safety circuit. Requires specialized high-pot testing apparatus.

SEVERITY: HIGH • CERTIFIED SPECIAL TOOL REQUIRED
device_thermostat
3. Battery Cell Imbalance > 8°C

Telemetry logs show individual module delta temperatures exceeding 8.0°C under DC fast charge simulation, implying localized chiller block blockage within the sealed battery tray assembly.

SEVERITY: PACK REPAIR • AUTHORIZED DEALER LEVEL
front_hand
4. Absence of Class 0 Rated Tooling

Technician lacks certified Class 0 (1,000V) electrical insulating gloves, arc-flash face shield, or CAT IV insulated multimeter leads. Proceeding without mandatory PPE violates OSHA / EN 50110 standards.

SEVERITY: WORKSHOP SAFETY PROTOCOL STOP
