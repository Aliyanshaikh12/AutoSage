01

Plain-Language Technical Summary

Subsystem Dossier

The proportional coolant bypass valve modulates refrigerant-to-glycol chiller loop exchange across the J1-II 800V battery enclosure. When the mechanical rotor jams or its internal feedback potentiometer drifts, low-temperature coolant fails to route through the auxiliary battery chiller heat exchanger under rapid DC charging (350kW) or high-speed sustained autobahn loads.

As battery thermal inertia reaches capacity, the thermal management ECU initiates progressive recuperation torque de-rates and limits battery pack thermal dissipation to prevent localized lithium plating.

Physical Inspection Protocol (Bench Verification)

Technician Checklist
01

High-Voltage Isolation Verification

Mandatory Safety Gate

Ensure 800V battery contactors are open and service disconnect plug (MSD) is removed. Verify zero voltage potential (<5V DC) on the auxiliary high-voltage interlock loop (HVIL) before technician access to the front lower chassis tray.

02

Actuator Connector Pinout Measurement

Connector X832

Disconnect plug X832 at the proportional 3-way valve housing. Using a calibrated digital multimeter, probe Pin 3 (PWM Command Signal) and Pin 4 (Ground Return). Nominal target during ignition-on active command is 12V ±0.5V reference square-wave.

Expected Coil Resistance (Pins 1 & 2): 18.5 Ω to 22.0 Ω @ 20°C
03

Mechanical Valve Rotor Freedom Check

Special Tool T40321

Manually depress mechanical bypass shaft using Porsche special tool T40321. Check for particulate binding or silicate crystallization from older G40/glycol additive interaction. Confirm smooth 90° radial articulation.

04

Vacuum Bleed & Refill Test

Automated Routine

Run PIWIS diagnostic routine: Thermal Management - Evacuation & Pressure Hold Cycle (-0.85 bar for 15 mins). Air pocket cavitation in battery Module Bank B mimics an actuator jam by starving the chiller exchanger.
